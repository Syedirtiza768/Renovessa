## 1. Strategic Understanding of the Four Verticals

### 1.1 Bathroom Remodeling

**Market dynamics:**
- High-intent, high-ticket remodel (typically $15K–$75K+). Homeowners research extensively before committing.
- Strong visual component — design inspiration, before/after, and layout planning drive early-funnel traffic.
- Local permit complexity varies significantly by jurisdiction (DC vs. Montgomery County vs. Fairfax County).
- Aging-in-place and accessibility upgrades are a growing segment in the DMV's aging homeowner population.
- Tub-to-shower conversions are the most common mid-range project.

**Search intent patterns:**
- **Informational (top funnel):** "bathroom remodeling ideas," "small bathroom layout," "walk-in shower designs"
- **Investigational (mid funnel):** "bathroom remodel cost Bethesda," "tub to shower conversion cost," "bathroom permits DC"
- **Transactional (bottom funnel):** "bathroom contractors near me," "get bathroom remodel quotes," "bathroom renovation estimate"

**Content differentiation for Renovessa:**
- The **bathroom planner** is Renovessa's most mature estimator tool. Content should naturally drive toward it.
- Focus on **scope-first education** — helping homeowners understand what they actually want before talking to contractors.
- Address **property-type constraints** (DC rowhouse narrow baths, condo HOA rules, NOVA townhouse shared walls).
- Provide **jurisdiction-specific permit guidance** that general national publishers get wrong.

**Strategic priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH. Mature product. Strong conversion potential. Competes with Houzz, Angi, Sweeten, and local design-build firms.

### 1.2 Solar

**Market dynamics:**
- Federal ITC (Investment Tax Credit) at 30% through 2032 makes solar financially compelling.
- Maryland and DC have additional SREC (Solar Renewable Energy Credit) markets that improve payback.
- Northern Virginia has net metering but fewer state-level incentives than MD/DC.
- HOA restrictions, roof age/condition, and shading are major disqualifiers.
- The solar customer journey is heavily research-driven — payback calculations, financing comparisons, and equipment choices dominate.
- Roof-integrated solar (building-integrated photovoltaics) is an emerging consideration for remodel projects.

**Search intent patterns:**
- **Informational:** "how does solar work," "solar panels pros and cons," "solar payback period"
- **Investigational:** "solar cost Northern Virginia," "solar panels cost calculator," "solar incentives Maryland"
- **Transactional:** "solar installers near me," "get solar quotes," "solar estimate"

**Content differentiation for Renovessa:**
- Solar is unique among the four verticals because it's **not a repair/replace decision** — it's an investment decision.
- Content must address **financial analysis** (payback, NPV, financing) alongside technical scope.
- The **solar planner** shows roof visualization and production estimates — content should reference this capability.
- Emphasize **DMV-specific incentive stacking** (federal ITC + state/utility programs) that national solar calculators often miss.
- Address the **roof-first question** — when to replace a roof before going solar.

**Strategic priority:** HIGH. Fastest-growing home improvement category. Strong policy tailwinds. Renovessa has a dedicated solar planner. Competes with EnergySage, local solar installers, and Tesla.

### 1.3 Roofing

**Market dynamics:**
- **Urgency-driven:** Leaks, storm damage, and visible deterioration create immediate demand.
- **Insurance-driven:** Many full replacements are insurance claims (hail, wind, fallen trees).
- **Seasonal:** Spring and fall are peak seasons. Post-storm surges are unpredictable.
- **Material diversity:** Asphalt shingles (dominant), metal, slate, tile, flat membrane, cedar shake.
- **DC-area specific:** Historic district restrictions (especially in DC and Old Town Alexandria), flat roofs on rowhouses, complex NOVA suburban rooflines.
- **High fraud risk:** Storm-chasing contractors and insurance scams make trust/verification especially important.

**Search intent patterns:**
- **Urgency/problem:** "roof leak repair," "emergency roof repair," "storm damage roof"
- **Investigational:** "roof replacement cost Maryland," "how long does a roof last," "roofing materials comparison"
- **Transactional:** "roofing contractors near me," "roof estimate," "get roof quotes"

**Content differentiation for Renovessa:**
- Roofing is the **highest-trust category** — a bad roof job is catastrophic. Emphasize credential verification and insurance-claim guidance.
- Address the **repair vs. full replacement** decision with clear criteria.
- Cover **DMV storm patterns** (hurricane remnants, derechos, hail) and insurance claim processes.
- Address **historic district and HOA constraints** specific to the DMV.
- The **flat roof / low-slope** segment is significant in DC and needs dedicated content.

**Strategic priority:** HIGH. Essential service. High urgency = high conversion. Strong local SEO opportunity. Competes with Angi, local roofing companies, and insurance-adjacent services.

---

### 1.4 HVAC

**Market dynamics:**
- **Urgency-driven in extremes:** AC failure in July, furnace failure in January = emergency demand.
- **Seasonal maintenance:** Spring AC tune-ups, fall furnace checks create predictable demand.
- **Energy efficiency push:** Heat pumps are heavily incentivized (federal tax credits, utility rebates) and increasingly mandated in some jurisdictions.
- **Repair vs. replace is the core decision:** Age, efficiency, refrigerant phase-out (R-22 → R-410A → next-gen), and repair frequency drive this.
- **Equipment complexity:** SEER ratings, HSPF, variable-speed compressors, dual-fuel systems — homeowners need translation.
- **DMV climate specifics:** Hot, humid summers; cold winters; heat pumps viable but sizing matters.

**Search intent patterns:**
- **Urgency/problem:** "AC not working," "furnace not heating," "emergency HVAC repair"
- **Investigational:** "HVAC replacement cost," "heat pump vs gas furnace," "AC unit cost"
- **Transactional:** "HVAC contractors near me," "get AC quotes," "HVAC estimate"

**Content differentiation for Renovessa:**
- HVAC is the **recommended first wedge** per the 2026-07-23 strategy document.
- Fairfax County / Northern Virginia should be the **primary geographic depth**.
- Address the **heat pump transition** — this is the biggest story in HVAC right now and highly search-relevant.
- Cover **refrigerant transitions** (R-22 phase-out complete, R-410A being phased down) and what they mean for repair vs. replace.
- Emphasize **load calculation and proper sizing** — an oversized system is worse than an undersized one in the DMV's humid climate.

**Strategic priority:** HIGHEST. Recommended first wedge. Strong operational coverage. Year-round demand. Competes with Angi, local HVAC companies, and utility rebate programs.

## 2. Cross-Vertical Content Strategy

### 2.1 Content Tier Architecture

For each vertical, we publish in three tiers:

| Tier | Description | Volume | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hub** | Service-level landing page with full scope, cost context, and CTAs | 1 per vertical | `/services/hvac/` |
| **Pillar** | Comprehensive guides (3,000–5,000 words) covering cost, permits, comparisons | 3–5 per vertical | `/cost-guides/hvac-replacement-cost-northern-virginia/` |
| **Cluster** | Targeted articles (1,500–2,500 words) on specific problems, decisions, locations | 5–10 per vertical | `/resources/ac-blowing-warm-air-northern-virginia/` |

### 2.2 The Inter-Vertical Bridge

Four verticals create natural cross-selling content:

| Bridge Topic | Primary Vertical | Secondary Vertical | Content Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roof condition before solar | Solar | Roofing | "Should you replace your roof before installing solar panels?" |
| Bathroom exhaust ventilation | Bathroom | HVAC | "Proper bathroom ventilation: fan sizing, ductwork, and moisture control" |
| Attic insulation + HVAC | HVAC | Roofing | "How attic insulation affects HVAC sizing and efficiency" |
| Whole-home energy audit | Solar | HVAC | "The case for a whole-home energy audit before solar or HVAC upgrades" |
| Storm damage scope | Roofing | Bathroom | "When a roof leak damages your bathroom: coordinating repairs" |

These bridges live in `/resources/` and build topical authority across the home-improvement domain.

### 2.3 The Trust Stack (Applied to All Four)

Every article must visibly stack:

1. **Scope education** — What does this project actually involve?
2. **Local context** — How do DC, Maryland, and Northern Virginia differ?
3. **Cost transparency** — What drives price, without false precision?
4. **Permit/credential guidance** — Who needs to be licensed, and where?
5. **Clear next step** — Calculate my range / Build my scope / Compare bids.

## 5. Content Quality Standards

### 5.1 Every Article Must Include

1. **Unique title tag** — `[Specific Topic] in [Location] — [Guide Type] | Renovessa`
2. **Unique meta description** — Value proposition with next action
3. **H1** — Matches or closely paraphrases the title
4. **Date stack** — First published + last substantively reviewed
5. **Author + reviewer byline** — Named where possible, role otherwise
6. **Methodology disclosure** — How cost ranges are constructed
7. **Required disclaimer** — Planning estimate, not a contractor quote
8. **Internal links** — Parent hub, location hub, methodology, related guides, estimator
9. **Primary CTA** — Contextual variant of "Estimate my project"
10. **Sources** — Official permit sources, licensed reviewers, or "insufficient Renovessa data yet"

### 5.2 Content Must Never

- Promise exact prices, guaranteed savings, or specific payback periods without modeling
- Claim "best" or "top-rated" contractors without published methodology
- Publish unverified statistics (appointments, confirmation rates, ZIP coverage)
- Give legal advice (permit determination is always "likely required" or "consult your jurisdiction")
- Invent case studies, quotes, prices, or contractor names
- Duplicate content across city pages (substantial uniqueness gate applies)

### 5.3 Voice and Tone

- **Expert but accessible** — No jargon without explanation
- **Local and specific** — "In Fairfax County" not "in your area"
- **Transparent, not salesy** — Lead with education, close with CTA
- **Empathetic to homeowner anxiety** — Home improvement is stressful and expensive
- **Confident about methodology** — When we have data, we explain how we got it

## 6. Measurement Framework

### 6.1 Leading Indicators (Weeks 1–4)
- Pages indexed in Google Search Console
- Average position for target queries
- Click-through rate from SERP
- Time on page and scroll depth

### 6.2 Lagging Indicators (Months 2–6)
- Organic sessions by vertical
- Estimator starts attributed to content pages
- RFQ submissions from content-originated traffic
- Content-assisted conversions (last-click + multi-touch)

### 6.3 Content Refresh Cycle
- **Quarterly:** Cost guides (materials and labor rates change)
- **Semiannually:** Permit guides (jurisdiction rules change)
- **Annually:** Methodology, comparison guides, problem guides
- **As needed:** When Search Console shows query cannibalization or ranking drops

The actual article content templates for all four verticals are stored in:

- `src/lib/content-templates/bathroom.ts` — Bathroom remodeling templates

```typescript

  slug: string;
  title: string;
  bodyText: string;
```

A unified seed script at `scripts/seed-content-templates.ts` handles all verticals.
